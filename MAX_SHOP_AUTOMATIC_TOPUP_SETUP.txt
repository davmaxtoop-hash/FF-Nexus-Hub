MAX SHOP — AUTOMATIC FREE FIRE TOP-UP

Flow:
Customer enters Free Fire UID -> selects diamonds -> Paystack payment -> server verifies Paystack -> GameCore creates UID top-up -> server polls delivery status -> order becomes Fulfilled when GameCore reports completed.

Provider used by this build: GameCore B2B direct UID top-up. Their documentation describes Free Fire as an id_only product flow and requires a server-side X-Api-Key.

Railway environment variable required:
GAMECORE_API_KEY=your_live_gamecore_api_key

Optional:
GAMECORE_BASE_URL=https://api.gamecore-api.tech

The MAX SHOP package names are matched to GameCore Free Fire products by the diamond amount (for example 100 Diamonds -> product whose amountType.value is 100). The product's deliveryDataSchema is read before ordering so the correct Player ID field is used.

IMPORTANT:
1. A real GameCore account/API key is required. The ZIP cannot contain a real provider credential.
2. Your GameCore account must have Free Fire UID top-up products available for your region.
3. Paystack payment does not itself deliver diamonds; GameCore is the fulfillment provider.
4. Buyers must confirm their UID because a wrong UID can receive the top-up and may not be reversible.
5. Never put GAMECORE_API_KEY in index.html or other frontend files.
